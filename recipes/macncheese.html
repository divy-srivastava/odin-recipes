Mac n Cheese

Description

Mac n Cheese is a classic comfort food made with elbow macaroni and a creamy cheese sauce. It's a simple yet delicious dish that's perfect for a quick meal.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni according to the package instructions.
  2. In a saucepan, melt the butter and whisk in the flour.
  3. Gradually add the milk and whisk until smooth.
  4. Cook for 2-3 minutes or until thickened.
  5. Remove from heat and stir in the cheese until melted.
  6. Add salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Combine the cheese sauce with the cooked macaroni.
